# DemocInfo

## About the Project

DemocInfo is a customer retention and engagement platform designed to help companies understand customer behavior and take more effective actions to prevent customer loss.

Acquiring new customers is already a major challenge for companies. However, keeping those customers active over time can be even more difficult. Customers may access an application, make a purchase, or use a service once and never return.

DemocInfo aims to solve this problem by analyzing customer behavior and identifying changes in usage patterns, allowing companies to understand when a customer may be losing interest and determine the most appropriate action to take.

The main idea is simple: **instead of treating every customer the same, understand each customer's behavior and act at the right time.**

## Problem

Companies often have large amounts of customer data, but simply having data does not mean knowing what to do with it.

For example, imagine a customer who normally makes a purchase every 10 days. If 20 days have passed without a new purchase, this customer may be showing a change in behavior.

The company could send a discount, free shipping, a special offer, or another incentive. However, sending discounts to every customer can generate unnecessary costs, especially for customers who would purchase anyway.

On the other hand, doing nothing may result in losing customers.

DemocInfo addresses this problem by helping companies identify behavioral changes and determine when an intervention may be necessary.

## Objective

The main objective of DemocInfo is to transform customer data into practical retention strategies.

The system aims to:

* Analyze customer behavior
* Identify changes in usage and purchasing patterns
* Detect customers who may be at risk of leaving
* Classify customers according to their risk level
* Recommend personalized actions
* Help companies choose the right moment to act
* Measure the results of retention strategies
* Improve customer engagement and retention

## How It Works

The DemocInfo process can be divided into four main steps:

### 1. Data Collection

The system receives customer information and behavioral data from the company's systems.

### 2. Behavioral Analysis

DemocInfo analyzes customer activity and identifies patterns, changes, and unusual behavior.

### 3. Risk Identification

The system identifies customers whose behavior may indicate a higher probability of becoming inactive or leaving.

### 4. Recommended Action

Based on the analysis, DemocInfo suggests possible actions, such as:

* Personalized discounts
* Special offers
* Notifications
* Loyalty benefits
* Free shipping
* Promotional campaigns
* Other retention strategies

The company remains responsible for deciding which actions should actually be implemented.

## Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is one of the main components of DemocInfo.

The AI can assist in:

* Identifying behavioral patterns
* Detecting changes in customer activity
* Estimating customer risk
* Segmenting customers
* Identifying potential churn
* Recommending retention strategies
* Helping determine the appropriate time to contact a customer

The goal is not to replace the company's decisions, but to provide useful information that allows decisions to be made based on customer behavior and data.

## Main Features

* Customer behavior analysis
* Customer risk classification
* Churn risk identification
* Personalized recommendations
* Retention campaign management
* Customer segmentation
* Performance monitoring
* Data-driven decision making
* Results tracking

## Conclusion

DemocInfo aims to help companies move from simply storing customer data to actually using that data to make better decisions.

By analyzing individual customer behavior, identifying potential risks, and recommending appropriate actions, the platform can help companies improve retention, reduce customer churn, and create stronger long-term relationships with their customers.

**DemocInfo: Understand your customers. Act at the right time. Retain more.**
